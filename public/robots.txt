# Everyone is welcome, and the AI crawlers are welcome by name.
#
# That is a decision, not an oversight. The point of this site is to be what an assistant
# finds when someone asks about Tom Butler's work, so the crawlers that feed those answers
# are invited in explicitly rather than left to the wildcard.
#
# These crawlers fetch HTML and do not execute JavaScript. That is why every route is
# prerendered at build time: without real markup in the body there is nothing here for
# them to read, and a client-rendered site is an empty div to all of them.
